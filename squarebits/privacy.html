SquareBits — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

This privacy policy applies to the SquareBits app (the "Application") for Android devices, created by Raxden Studios (the "Service Provider") as a free service. It explains what information the Application collects, why, and what choices you have.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required, and the Application does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (puzzles, scores, streaks, missions, trophies and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The Application uses Google Firebase to measure how it is used, to report crashes and to deliver notifications. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold.
  • The Application does not show ads.

Information stored on your device

Puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, missions, trophies, preferences and similar game data are saved locally on your device so the game can work offline. This data is not sent to the Service Provider. It is removed when you clear the Application's data or uninstall it.

Information collected automatically

The Application uses the following third-party services from Google, which collect some information automatically:

ServiceData collectedPurpose
Firebase Analytics App instance identifier, in-app events (for example screens visited, puzzles started or completed), app version, device model, operating system version, language and approximate country derived from the IP address Understanding how the Application is used in order to improve it
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating system version, app version and an installation identifier Detecting and fixing bugs and stability problems
Firebase Cloud Messaging A device registration token Delivering notifications (for example the daily challenge reminder)

The Application does not collect precise or approximate location from your device's location services, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to send the analytics, crash and notification data described above.
  • Notifications: used to show reminders and game notifications. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings; the game keeps working without it.

How the information is used and shared

The information described above is used only to operate, maintain and improve the Application. It is processed by Google as a service provider on behalf of the Service Provider. It is not sold and is not shared with third parties for advertising.

The Service Provider may disclose information if required by law, for example to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, or investigate fraud.

Google's handling of this data is described in its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

Data retention

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Data stored on your device remains there until you clear the Application's data or uninstall it.

Your choices

  • You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application.
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • You can reset your device's advertising and app identifiers from your Android settings.
  • You can ask the Service Provider to delete data associated with your installation by writing to the address below.

Children

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider so that it can be deleted.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit. The Service Provider takes reasonable measures to protect the information processed through the Application, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. You are advised to review it periodically; continued use of the Application after changes means you accept them.

Contact

If you have any questions about privacy in the Application, please contact the Service Provider at raxden.dev@gmail.com.
